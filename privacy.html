1. Who We Are and Scope

VoxStarion ("we", "us") is an independent game development studio. This policy describes how we handle information in connection with (a) this website and (b) the games we publish. It applies uniformly to all of our games.

The website and the games may have different data practices. Where a game collects or shares information differently from what is described here, an app-specific privacy disclosure will be provided and linked from that game's store listing.

2. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little information as necessary. The categories below apply uniformly to our website and all of our games.

Information you provide directly

  • Messages you send us when you contact support (for example, your email address and the contents of your message).

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app diagnostics — for example, device model, operating system version, app version, and crash diagnostics — where technically necessary to operate the game and fix issues. This applies uniformly across all our games.

Information from third-party SDKs

  • We do not currently integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs into our games. If we add any SDK in the future, we will update this policy and list the SDK and the data it collects.

3. How Information Is Used

Information described above is used to:

  • operate and maintain our games and website;
  • respond to support requests and feedback;
  • understand aggregate usage so we can improve our games;
  • detect, prevent, and address technical issues and abuse.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Our games do not currently display third-party advertisements and do not use third-party analytics SDKs. If we introduce advertising or analytics in any game, we will update this policy beforehand and identify the providers and their privacy disclosures.

5. Children's Privacy and Age-Appropriate Experiences

Our games are designed for a general audience. They are not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If any future game is directed at children, we will apply additional age-appropriate measures and describe them here.

If you believe a child has provided us personal information without parental consent, contact us so we can address it.

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share game data with third parties for advertising or marketing. We share information only as needed to operate our games and website — for example, with hosting providers that process data on our behalf — and where required by law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, then delete or anonymize it. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and handle follow-ups. To request deletion of information associated with you, contact us using the details in section 12.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, such as limiting access and using reputable service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

This website is served via a global content delivery network, so technical data from website visits may be processed in different regions. We do not otherwise transfer your information internationally.

10. User Rights and Requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us as described in section 12. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our games and practices evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and where appropriate, through in-app or store-listing notices.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@voxstarion.my.id (see also our contact page).
