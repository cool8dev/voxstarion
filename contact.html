Before you write in

To help us help you faster, please include:

  • The game name (e.g. Letter Rain) and app version, if you know it
  • Your device model and Android version
  • A short description of the issue and the steps to reproduce it
  • Screenshots, if they help explain the problem

Please never send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal data by email.

Email addresses are configured by the site owner in assets/js/config.js. There is intentionally no web contact form — we don't operate a form backend, and we won't pretend to.
